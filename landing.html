Core features

Not just an app.
A companion.

MirAI goes beyond simple tracking. It learns who you are and becomes smarter with every conversation.

🧠
Persistent AI memory

MirAI remembers your preferences, past conversations, and emotional reactions. Every interaction makes it smarter about you.

✦
Deep recommendations

Not just genres — MirAI matches by emotional tone, pacing, themes, and your current mood. Recommendations that actually fit.

◎
AI companion chat

Talk about what you're watching, how you feel, what you want next. MirAI listens, understands, and responds intelligently.

Tell MirAI you're stressed, excited, or bored — and it curates a perfect list for exactly how you feel right now.
